How it works

Simple onboarding in four steps

Poulex follows a guided implementation – from farm registration to device installation and training – so your team can start using the system in days, not months.

1
Farm & batch setup

We register your farm, houses and batches; set basic parameters like bird type, cycle length and production targets.

2
Module selection

You choose which modules to start with – farm data, health, IoT – and we configure access for farm staff and supervisors.

3
Deploy & train

Our team installs IoT devices (if needed), trains your team on mobile/desktop use and checks data quality for the first cycles.

4
Monitor & scale

Review dashboards, track KPIs and gradually add more sheds, more batches or partner farms as you grow.
